# Voltra Official Terms of Sale

## Orders and payment
Prices include all product costs shown at checkout. Sales tax is calculated at checkout. We charge your PayPal account once when your order ships.

## Shipping
Orders ship within 2 business days. Tracking is emailed when the parcel leaves our warehouse.

## Returns
Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Return shipping is free for defective items.

## Warranty
All Voltra products include a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Renewed products carry a 90-day warranty.

## Contact
Questions about an order? Reply to your confirmation email and our support team answers within one business day.
