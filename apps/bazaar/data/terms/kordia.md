# Kordia Audio Terms of Sale

## Orders and payment
Prices are shown in USD. Sales tax is calculated at checkout. Your PayPal account is charged when your order ships.

## Shipping
Free standard shipping on all orders. Orders ship within 1 business day.

## Returns
Earbuds can be returned within 30 days if the ear tips are unused. Refreshed products can be returned within 14 days.

## KordiaCloud sync
Some Kordia products include KordiaCloud sync, which keeps your EQ and ANC profiles in step across devices. KordiaCloud starts with a free 30-day trial. After the trial, the subscription automatically renews and converts to a paid plan at $9.99/month, charged to the payment method used for your order, unless you cancel in the Kordia app before the trial ends.

## Warranty
All Kordia earbuds include a 12-month limited warranty.
