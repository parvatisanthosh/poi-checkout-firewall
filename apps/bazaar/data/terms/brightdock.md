# Brightdock Terms of Sale

## Orders and payment
Prices are shown in USD. Sales tax is calculated at checkout.

## Shipping
Free shipping on orders over $25; otherwise $2.99.

## Returns
Returns are accepted within 30 days. Opened items are subject to a 20% restocking fee. Warehouse Second items are final sale.

## HubShield Protection Plans
HubShield plans are optional accident protection plans sold with Brightdock hubs. Plans can be cancelled within 30 days for a full refund.

## Warranty
Brightdock products include a 12-month limited warranty.
