# Lumen&Co Terms of Sale

## Orders and payment
Prices are shown in USD and include product costs only. Sales tax is calculated at checkout.

## Shipping
Free shipping in the contiguous US. Orders ship within 2 business days.

## Returns
Return any unused product within 30 days for a full refund.

## DeskCare
DeskCare is Lumen's posture coaching service in the Lumen app. Bundles that include DeskCare give you the first month free. After the first month, DeskCare is billed every 30 days at $4.99 to your original payment method until you cancel from your Lumen account.

## Warranty
Lumen stands carry a 24-month warranty; Lumen earbuds carry a 12-month warranty.
